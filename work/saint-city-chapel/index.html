Saint City Chapel

Worship leading and church production · 2025–2026

Overview

I joined Saint City Chapel in August 2025 with no worship leading experience. Within months I was leading worship at Salt Company, the Thursday college ministry, and I became the primary worship leader for that service.

At the same time I found gaps in production and built what a young church plant now runs at a high level. I led a six-person team on a Yamaha DM7, Shure wireless, Dante, Blackmagic, and ProPresenter stack, and wrote an SOP that standardized the workflow across both services.

I also designed merch for the church and for Salt Company, from the first sketch through a local printer in Tempe. The pieces are worn across the congregation and the college ministry.

Worship leading

I could play guitar and sing. I had never stood in front of a room and led. Salt Company draws 80 to 90 ASU students on Thursday nights. I plan every service: the songs, the transitions, the arc of the night, and where spoken moments land.

By early 2026, Ronnie handed Salt worship to me. I own every Thursday, and I lead Sunday mornings as well.

Live production

Under the service is a Yamaha DM7 Compact with a Rio3224-D over Dante, Shure QLXD wireless, Blackmagic BMPCC 6K cameras, DaVinci Resolve, and ProPresenter. I coordinate the six-person team across Sunday and Thursday Salt: scheduling, direction, and making sure nothing breaks when it matters.

The team was running on memory. Everyone knew the job, and nothing was written down. I wrote a standard operating procedure for this specific setup. It is the reference for every service.

Every Sunday sermon and every Salt message goes out as a podcast on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube, organized by series and season. Just under 50 episodes.

Apparel

I designed the lines and owned the path to a printer: research, calls, vetting, and production. We landed on a local shop in Tempe. Roughly 300 pieces, worn across Salt Company and Saint City.

Salt had three drops. A launch tee, SALT ASU. A camo hoodie with SALT OF THE EARTH running along one arm, across the chest, and down the other. And a Thursdays are for Salt design, for the weekly rhythm of the ministry.

For the church I designed a boxy jersey, two hats, a polo, a tee, a kids tee, and a kids serve-team shirt. The aim was clothes people actually want to wear, not a merch table.

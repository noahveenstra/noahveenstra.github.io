Instagram Posting UI

Concept · Product design · 2024

Overview

Instagram keeps reinventing itself without losing the people who showed up for the original product. Stories, Reels, shopping, DMs as a primary way to talk. The growth is real, and it has a cost.

The posting screen is the most-used action in the app, and it has become a visual argument between features. Common tasks sit next to rare ones with the same weight. The screen slows people down at the moment they are trying to do something simple.

The problem

The existing screen surfaces everything at once: alt text, scheduling, fundraising, audience, Facebook cross-posting, reminders. All of it asks for attention before you can post a photo.

Most people do not need a fundraiser or a scheduled post on a given day. Those options still take the same space as the ones used every time. More settings sit below the frame, so you cannot see the whole decision without scrolling. The screen teaches hunting instead of deciding.

The redesign

Decide what belongs on the primary screen, and move the rest behind a toolbar that opens with one tap.

The surface shows only the decisions people make on nearly every post. Scheduling, fundraisers, audience changes, and cross-posting collapse into a second layer. It is there if you need it, and it is out of the way if you do not. People who never schedule a post never see it. People who do can find it every time, in one tap.

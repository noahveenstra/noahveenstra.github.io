Instagram Posting UI

Project

Concept · Product design

Year

2024

I explored how Instagram’s posting screen might better balance a growing number of options with the task of creating a post. This independent 2024 concept began with my own assessment of the interface; it was not commissioned by Instagram.

In the version of Instagram I reviewed in 2024, Immediately, we see a full page of options and it feels busy. Oh, and at the bottom, there’s a separate page for even more settings. Some settings appeared on a separate page. This was the fundamental issue for me. I wanted to explore whether more options could remain accessible in context. Not to mention you can’t even see the post because of how many options and how they’re laid out. I also wondered whether some options could take less prominent space for people who rarely need them. For example, scheduling, fundraisers, audience controls, and sharing to Facebook competed for space alongside the post itself. These features can be important; my assessment of how often people use them was an assumption that would need validation. From this framework, I created a new design for this screen specifically.

My idea was simple, strip down to essential features and create a toolbar for the more superfluous options that would expand on tap and allow for customization. The intent was to reduce visual clutter while keeping additional options accessible. The tradeoff is that the extra tap could make some settings less discoverable; the concept would need user testing to determine whether it actually helps people post more easily.

Pursuit Intelligence

Project stories are the weight-bearing part of an AEC proposal: what happened in the field, how the team worked with the owner and architect, how trades actually coordinated. Those stories were never cataloged well. Finding one meant calling a PM or superintendent and hoping they remembered — or getting pointed to another proposal someone had already written.

I turned 1,334 proposal records, thousands of project views, and a structured people/strategy graph into a private local library I could query in minutes. Nine of those projects are authorized stories; the rest stay leads until they earn that status. The design that mattered was not search. It was the rule that a convenient hit cannot become a stronger claim than the source supports. IT is now taking the model national.

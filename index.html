Pursuit Intelligence

Project stories are the weight-bearing part of an AEC proposal: what happened in the field, how the team worked with the owner and architect, how trades actually coordinated. Those stories were never cataloged well. Finding one meant calling a PM or superintendent and hoping they remembered — or getting pointed to another proposal someone had already written.

I ingested 2.5 years of proposals into a local markdown corpus and a structured relationship layer that linked projects, people, teams, and business units. Marketing could query that graph and get a specific, grounded story in a few minutes. Other teams started coming to marketing when they needed something ultra-specific. IT is now taking the model national.
